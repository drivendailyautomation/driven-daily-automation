Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Driven Daily Automation is a personal desktop application designed to generate and prepare motivational video content.

Information We Process

The application currently processes video scripts, audio, and video files locally on the owner's computer.

TikTok Integration

TikTok integration is under development. Publishing is currently disabled. If TikTok authorization is enabled in the future, account access will require the account owner's permission.

Data Sharing

The application does not currently send account information to TikTok through its own integration. Future integrations may transmit authorized content and necessary account information to TikTok for publishing.

Data Retention

Generated content is stored locally and can be deleted by the application owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact @drivendailymotivation on TikTok.
